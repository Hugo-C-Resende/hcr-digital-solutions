Frequently asked questions

Is HCR Finance Master free?

Yes, during the beta. No card is requested.

Can I use it for a family budget?

Yes. You can manage the household finances and invite other people to the same household.

Does it connect to my bank?

No. You import CSV statements; the file is read on your device and only the imported transactions are synced with your account.

Is it a virtual wallet (e-wallet)?

No. It does not store cards or make payments: it is an expense-tracking and personal-finance app.

Can I delete my account and data?

Yes. In Settings → Privacy you can export your transactions as CSV and delete the account with your password.

Which devices does it run on?

On Windows (Microsoft Store) and in any modern browser, including mobile.
