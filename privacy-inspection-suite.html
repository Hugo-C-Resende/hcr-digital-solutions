3. Purposes and legal basis

  • Performance of a contract: account and work data, to provide the service you signed up for.
  • Legitimate interest: technical data, to keep the service stable and secure and to prevent abuse.
  • Consent: the optional connection to Google Calendar or Microsoft Outlook, which you can withdraw at any time.
  • Legal obligation: billing data, where it exists, to meet tax obligations.

We also use your email for transactional messages (for example, password recovery and confirmations of changes). We do not send marketing without your consent.

5. Where data is processed

The HCR Inspection Suite API, database and report generation are hosted on Render in the Oregon, United States (US West) region. This means app data is transferred outside the European Economic Area. These transfers rely on mechanisms provided for in the GDPR (such as standard contractual clauses or another appropriate safeguard). Other providers (Google, Microsoft, Stripe) may also process data outside the EEA under equivalent safeguards.

Video on the site: the HCR Inspection Suite demo is embedded in the product pages through YouTube (youtube-nocookie.com). The video loads only after you click play; until then your browser does not contact YouTube. After the click, YouTube (Google) may process your IP address and device data under Google's privacy policy. The app does not embed videos.

6. How long we keep data

We keep data while the account and organisation are active. You can request deletion of your account and of the organisation's data at any time at HCRFinanceMaster@gmail.com; we handle the request without undue delay and within one month at most, unless a legal retention obligation applies (for example, billing data, where it exists).

Backups. The database provider keeps backups for disaster recovery for a limited period that depends on the plan we use. Deleted data may remain in those backups until that period ends and is not used for any other purpose.

Technical logs. Server logs are kept only for the limited period set by the hosting provider.

7. Your rights

Under the GDPR, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we process about you and receive a copy, including in a structured format (portability).
  • Rectify incorrect data — directly in the app or on request.
  • Erase your data and restrict or object to processing — on request.
  • Withdraw consent at any time (for example, by disconnecting the calendar), without affecting earlier processing.
  • Lodge a complaint with the Portuguese data protection authority (CNPD) at www.cnpd.pt, or with the authority in your country.

Make requests by email to HCRFinanceMaster@gmail.com. If you are a user of a client organisation, some requests about work data may need to be forwarded to that organisation, which is responsible for that data.

8. Security

Connections to the app use encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS). Passwords are stored only as Argon2id hashes. Each organisation's data is isolated at database level (PostgreSQL Row-Level Security), not only in the application, and production access is restricted.

9. Minors

The service is intended for professional use and is not directed at people under 18.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The date of the last update is shown at the top and we will communicate material changes before they take effect.
